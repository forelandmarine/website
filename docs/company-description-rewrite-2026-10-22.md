Foreland Marine is an independent superyacht consultancy based in London, working for owners of sailing and motor yachts from 24 to 60 metres. We manage refits, represent owners on new builds, and provide yacht management.

The team includes captains, unlimited chief engineers and naval architects, with consultants in the main yachting centres. Our interests are directly aligned with the owner's by structure: no broker commissions, no referral fees.

On a refit we write the specification, tender the work, manage it on site and control the budget through to sea trials. On a new build we act for the owner from yard selection and contract through build supervision, delivery and the warranty period.
