# The owner's team: published competitor and industry material

Reviewed 7 November 2026 against /owners-representation/owners-team.

## Sources

SuperyachtNews, "The experts who comprise the ideal in-yard team" (refit example, EUR 12m budget). Nine roles: project manager/owner's representative holding a power of attorney and reporting via the owner's yacht managers; owner's yacht managers approving plan, budget and change orders; paint specialist, compliance specialist (class, flag, recertification) and technical specialists (tenders, toys, submarines, helicopters) all reporting to the project manager, the last "retained for only short periods"; legal representative from the family office or a law firm, liaising with the project manager; insurers approving works and contracts; interior designer liaising with owner, managers and project manager; financial representative "usually in the owner's office", paying the yard only with the owner's authorisation.
https://www.superyachtnews.com/operations/the-experts-who-comprise-the-ideal-in-yard-team

Moran Yacht & Ship, BOAT International advertorial (Sep 2026). "The client's chief representative and project manager"; one point of contact with "a much larger team working behind that person"; sits between client, shipyard, designer, attorney, family office, crew and consultants; integrates the owner's existing captain and advisers rather than replacing them; in-house engineering, quality-control and interior specialists; start from the brief, not the yard; continuity through warranty.
https://www.boatinternational.com/boat-presents/new-build-superyacht-guide-moran-yacht-ship-expert-advice

Penningtons Manches Cooper (Sarah Allan), "To be authorised - or not", SuperyachtNews, 25 Sep 2026. Representatives often have little or no authority to commit the owner, yet their decisions on variations and milestones can bind; a manager has no inherent authority by appointment; family offices often put time limits on powers of attorney; authority should be documented and checked.
https://www.superyachtnews.com/owner/to-be-authorised--or-not-

Superyacht Investor, "Mediator, translator, educator". Owner's representative as "figurehead of the owner's build team", distinct from the project manager, though one person may fill both on a small build; team for a 110m one-off differs from a 30m series boat; crew-accommodation gap traced to the representative not discussing operation with the owner.
https://www.superyachtinvestor.com/?p=18132

Fraser, "Building the dream". Core team named as broker, lawyer, owner's project manager, designer and shipyard; keep the core team small.
https://www.fraseryachts.com/en/news-detail/building-the-dream

MCM on Akira (57m Heesen). Retained before contract; reviewed specification and drawings and worked with the lawyers on the final specification and contract.
https://www.yachtsandyachting.com/news/273486/MCM-unveils-construction-insights-on-project-Akira

Burgess, Build a Yacht. Brokerage, technical services and management combined in one firm; no team structure or reporting lines published.
https://www.burgessyachts.com/en/build-a-yacht

Hill Robinson services guide: PDF not machine-readable; nothing usable.

## Where our page agrees

Single channel to the yard; specialists report through the representative and are retained for short periods; finance sits in the owner's office and pays on certification; lawyer engaged before contract; captain and crew involved during build; team assembled before yard selection; team scales with size.

## Gaps against published material

1. Owner-engaged designers (exterior, interior, naval architect of record) are not shown as team members. Fraser and SuperyachtNews both place the designer in the owner's team; our page mentions designers only where engaged by the yard.
2. No class and flag compliance role. SuperyachtNews lists a compliance specialist reporting to the project manager.
3. Representative and project manager not distinguished. Superyacht Investor notes they are separate roles that one person may combine on a small build; this fits the 24 to 35 m band.
4. Limits of authority could name the instrument: a letter of authority or power of attorney, with its scope and expiry checked (Penningtons).
5. Insurers are shown only as the broker's work; SuperyachtNews has underwriters approving contracts and waivers.
6. Fraser counts the broker within the core team. Our page places the broker outside it, on commission grounds; stated structurally, but a broker reader may object.
