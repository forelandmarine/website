# Google Business Profile description (22 October 2026)

Foreland Marine is an independent superyacht consultancy acting for yacht owners. We manage refits, represent owners on new builds, and provide yacht management and technical support for sailing and motor yachts from 24 to 60 metres.

Refit work covers specification, yard tendering, on-site project management, budget control and sea trials. New build representation runs from specification and yard selection through build supervision, delivery and the warranty period. Yacht management covers ISM compliance, crew, budgets, insurance, and class and flag administration.

Foreland Marine Consultancy Ltd is based at 7 Bell Yard, London, is registered on the YORR and is SYBAss accredited.
