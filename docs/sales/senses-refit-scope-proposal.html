The 30-year refit

M/Y Senses

Prepared for Captain Sebastian Cremades, M/Y Senses. From Jack MacNally, Foreland Marine. July 2026.

Following our exchange in July, and having given Senses some thought, I have set out below how we would take on her 30-year refit and what it would cost.

Senses is a renowned yacht, and a refit at this stage of her life is as much about preserving her character as it is about her construction and systems. Bringing a project manager in early, before the scope is written and the yard is chosen, is in our experience the single decision that most shapes how a project of this kind turns out. It is how we prefer to work, and it is why we have divided the engagement into two stages.

The first stage is the planning. Working alongside you, the Owner and the management team, we would establish a proper condition baseline against her 30-year survey and class requirements, develop and prioritise the scope of works, and build a realistic budget and programme, all before anything goes near a yard. We would then run the yard evaluation and take her to tender. You would come out of this stage holding an independent, fully costed plan, and free to place the work wherever it sits best.

The second stage is the refit itself. Once the yard is chosen and the work is placed, we would put a dedicated project manager on board through to sea trials and handover, on site day to day and reporting to you each week. Northern Europe suits this project well, and we hold long-standing relationships with yards in the Netherlands and Germany that are equal to a refit of this scale.

Throughout, we work owner-side only. We take no broker commissions and no supplier referral fees, so there is never a question about where our interest lies. It sits with the Owner, from the first meeting to the last.

Fees

The planning stage is a fixed fee of £25,000 plus VAT. That covers the condition survey and baseline, the scope development, the budget and programme, and the yard evaluation through to tender, together with our attendance where the work requires it. It is invoiced half on instruction and half on delivery of the plan.

The refit stage is billed on a straightforward rate card, so you pay only for the work the project actually needs:

Site attendance£550 per day
Consultancy£125 per hour
Administration£40 per hour

Travel, accommodation and any project expenses are charged at cost and agreed with you in advance. During the active yard period we would expect to be on site around twelve to fifteen days a month, which with the associated reporting and coordination puts the running cost somewhere between £9,000 and £12,000 a month. That is an indicative figure only. The precise programme, and with it the true monthly cost, is one of the things the planning stage exists to establish.

Terms

All figures are in pounds sterling and exclusive of VAT, which is charged in addition at the prevailing rate. Refit-stage time is invoiced monthly in arrears against a simple record of the days and hours worked, with expenses itemised alongside. This proposal holds for ninety days. Since the refit is not due until October 2029, we would set the timing of the planning stage to suit you and the Owner, ideally beginning twelve to eighteen months ahead of the yard period.

There is no urgency in deciding. Whenever your travels allow, I would welcome a call with you and the management team to talk it through.
