# Day-rate availability outreach

Purpose: turn on near-term cash by selling your own time at a day rate, and do it through marine work that keeps you in front of the people who give out mandates. This replaces non-marine temp work with paid hours that double as business development.

Keep it short, keep it confident, keep it specific. Availability, not need. Send individually, not as a visible group mail. Pick 15 to 20 names across captains, management companies, brokers, surveyors, and yard contacts and send this week.

### Notes for you (not for the recipients)

- **Rate.** Decide your number before you send, because someone will ask on the first reply. A defensible marketable day rate for owner-side attendance is in the £[500 to 750]/day range, travel at cost. Your internal £125/hr capitalisation rate is higher than the market will bear for a broadcast availability note, so anchor lower to get the first jobs and the relationships.
- **Tone.** "Capacity" and "availability," never "looking for work." In this market, availability reads as in demand, need reads as a risk.
- **Follow-through.** Every day of paid marine work is a business development day. Note who you meet on each job and add warm ones to the tracker.
- **Cadence.** Send in small batches, personalise the first line to each person, and follow up once after a week if there is no reply.
