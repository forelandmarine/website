# Search Console baseline, 10 September 2026

Raw export covering the three months to 8 September 2026, web search, forelandmarine.com. Kept so the October comparison is a diff against recorded numbers rather than a recollection.

The metadata fix went live on 9 September, the day before this export. That makes this the last clean reading of the old titles and descriptions, and the reason the next export matters.

The single number to compare: click-through rate across positions four to ten. It was 0.62 percent here, on 32,914 impressions and 203 clicks. If it has moved materially by 9 October the titles were the binding constraint. If it has not, answer absorption is structural and the informational estate needs a different scorecard.

Secondary comparisons worth making, in order:

Chapter-scale pages. The 10 percent rule article held 15,176 impressions at position 5.1 with 20 clicks. Any movement there is the clearest single signal, because it is the largest page by impressions and it sits in the middle of the affected band.

Question-shaped queries. "What is a typical seasonal yacht refit cost, and what factors..." held position 1.5 across 114 impressions with no clicks. If that is still zero in October, no title will ever fix it.

The refit page. /refit held 3,661 impressions at position 27.4 with 11 clicks, against a rewrite that went live on 9 September targeting "yacht refit project management", where the site holds 878 impressions at position 19.7. Position rather than click-through is the measure on that one.

The publication's insurance and depreciation pages, which did not exist at the time of this export and should appear in the next one.

A note on comparability. Google reports the bare domain and the www host separately for three URLs here, which understates www totals slightly. A domain-level property would remove that, and should be set up before the October export rather than after.
