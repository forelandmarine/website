# Watermans: brand and website plan

Prepared 21 September 2026, name and design settled the same day.
Companion to uk-agency-assessment.md.

## Decisions taken

Brand architecture: endorsed sister brand. Its own name, mark, domain and site, carrying a visible connection to Foreland Marine.

Scope claimed at launch: London and the Solent. Nothing wider until it can be delivered.

Fees: published figures, with a written at-cost pass-through commitment and no supplier rebates.

## Name

Watermans.

The Company of Watermen and Lightermen has licensed the men who move vessels on
the Thames since 1514. Watermen took ships in and out, carried people and goods
between the shore and vessels lying in the Pool, and knew the tide better than
the owners did. It is the same job with more paperwork, and the word is still
the working river's own.

Domains: watermansagency.com and watermansagency.co.uk, both unregistered when
checked on 21 September 2026, plus watermans.london. Bare watermans.co.uk and
watermansmarine.com are gone, which is why the agency suffix carries the name.

Two cautions. Never imply we hold a waterman's licence, because we do not. And
check the Company's own position before using any of its heraldry.

Names considered and set aside, with the reasoning, in case this is revisited:
Botolph Wharf, a legal quay of 1559 named for the patron saint of travellers.
Cherry Garden, where the PLA kept lookouts who telephoned Tower Bridge to have
it opened for an approaching ship. Wool Quay, where customs duty was paid from
1203 and Chaucer was Comptroller from 1374. Long Room, the room in the Custom
House where ships' business was transacted. Landward, Yantlet and Foreland
Agency from the earlier round.

Clipper was raised and rejected. On the Thames it means Thames Clippers, half of
London Cruise and Yacht, which holds the PLA contract for the three central
moorings we book every berth through. In the Solent it means Clipper Ventures at
Gosport. The word is occupied twice over in exactly our two cruising grounds.

## Positioning

The category is competing on warmth, heritage and stock photographs of Tower Bridge. Nobody is competing on precision.

The proposition is a single sentence: the UK arrival handled by people who can also fix what is broken when you get here.

Three supports, in the order they matter to a captain.

First, published fees and at-cost disbursements. Every supplier invoice passed through at cost, no rebates, no percentage, funds requested in advance against a pro-forma account. MGMT publishes a pricing page with no figures on it. Publishing real numbers takes ground nobody currently holds, and it is the only claim in this market that cannot be answered with a better photograph.

Second, technical attendance. Anyone can order a pilot. Almost no UK agent can also put an engineer aboard, arrange class or flag attendance alongside, hold the Temporary Admission position, or supervise a contractor during a five day stop. This is the link back to Foreland Marine and the reason the work is worth doing at all.

Third, correspondent standing. A named UK contact for overseas agents and management companies, working to a pro-forma account and a written reporting format they recognise.

## Visual identity

Watermans is a Foreland Group company and looks like one. The first attempt
treated it as a standalone brand with its own chart-document language, which
was wrong on every count: not premium enough for the market, wrong colours,
flat imagery, and no visible relationship to the parent.

### The group system, carried directly

Palette, from the Foreland Marine brand kit: Deep Navy #040D1A, Navy #081630,
Dark Blue #0C1E42, Ocean Blue #5386B6 as the accent, Muted Blue #7BA8C8 for
body copy, white reserved for headings. Dark throughout, never a light page.

Typography: Nunito Sans, with Light 300 for headings as the signature weight,
400 for body and 600 for labels and buttons.

Behaviours carried across: the radial glow, the white/10 horizon line, the
accent bar on cards, solid accent primary buttons with white/30 ghost
outlines, and section labels in accent caps at widest tracking.

A captain who has seen forelandmarine.com should recognise this site as the
same firm before reading a word of it.

### What makes it Watermans rather than Foreland

The mark. Code flag H, I have a pilot on board, drawn as line art at the
weight of the Foreland lighthouse so the two marks sit together in a group
lockup. Not a call for help but the state of being looked after, which is what
the agency sells.

The lockup mirrors the parent exactly: mark, then WATERMANS in letterspaced
caps, with YACHT AGENCY beneath in the position Foreland uses for MARINE
CONSULTANCY.

### Cinematic, not documentary

Full bleed night hero with parallax and a left scrim so the headline sits on a
dark field. Alternating split panels down the page where the photograph carries
the weight and the copy sits beside it. The arrival sequence as accent barred
tiles rather than a ruled table.

### Photography

Placeholder at present, drawn from the Foreland Marine library, and none of it
is London. Before launch this needs real Thames material: a yacht lying on a
central tier, Tower Bridge raised, and a yacht alongside at St Katharine Docks.
Commission it or licence it, but do not launch on Mediterranean stock for a
London agency.

## Voice

Foreland's external copy register applies, with one shift. The consultancy voice explains. The agency voice instructs.

Short sentences. Times, distances and figures stated plainly. No adjectives about service quality, because every competitor uses them and none of them mean anything. Where a rule exists, cite it and date it. Where a figure is unconfirmed, say so.

The test for any sentence on the site: would it survive being read aloud on a bridge at 0600.

## Website

Eight pages, static, no CMS. The site is a reference work with a telephone number on it.

### 1. Home

What we do, where, and the three supports. The arrival chain diagram. Published fee summary with a link to the full tariff. The 24 hour number, visible without scrolling. No carousel, no hero video, no testimonials at launch because there are none.

### 2. Arriving in London

The page the whole site is built around, and the reason anyone will find it.

Pilotage compulsory over 40m and how the provisional order to London Port Control works. The three PLA tiers and that all of them, with mooring, refuse, black, grey and fresh water, are booked through London Cruise and Yacht on 48 hours notice. Tower Bridge lifts, 24 hours notice, 9m air draught, no charge, requested by the agent, owner or master. St Katharine Docks as the only lock-in option and its 40 to 42m ceiling. Tide windows and air draught. Where the Port of London begins.

Written as an operational guide, not a sales page. If a captain can plan an arrival from this page without calling us, the page is working, because the next yacht that does call will call us.

### 3. Arriving in the Solent

Ocean Village, Shamrock Quay, Saxon Wharf, Gunwharf Quays, Poole. Maximum LOA, lift capacity, road access, what each is actually good for. Honest about where we are booking rather than attending.

### 4. Clearance and compliance

sPCR timing, Border Force, the National Yachtline fallback. Temporary Admission and the 18 month position. The 2026 ETA requirement and the Seafarer Transit Scheme exemption for professional crew. Fuel, including the point that red diesel remains available to private pleasure craft in Great Britain on a duty declaration and that the propulsion ban applies in Northern Ireland, which most of the category gets wrong.

Every item dated and sourced. This page is the AEO asset.

### 5. Fees

Actual figures. Proposed structure below, for sign-off before publication.

| Agency fee, 30 to 45m | 1,250 |

All figures in pounds, excluding VAT. The agency fee covers pre-arrival planning, all authority notifications, clearance, berth booking, pilot ordering and attendance on arrival and departure.

Stated on the same page, in the same size type as the numbers: disbursements are passed through at cost against supplier invoices, we take no rebate or commission from any supplier, and cleared funds are required in advance against a pro-forma account.

Confirm VAT treatment of disbursements with the accountant before this page goes live.

### 6. Technical attendance

The differentiator, and the page that carries the link to forelandmarine.com. Engineer attendance, class and flag surveys alongside, contractor supervision during a stop, warranty and snagging, ISM support. Written for a captain with a problem and five days in port.

### 7. For agents and managers

The correspondent proposition. How we work: single named contact, pro-forma disbursement account, funds in advance, agreed reporting format, no rebates taken from your suppliers. Primary outreach target is the network offices that have no UK presence.

### 8. Contact

One 24 hour number, obtained separately and answered in season. Not a personal mobile. WhatsApp, email, and the company position stated plainly, including that the agency is part of Foreland Marine.

### Also

A one page UK arrival checklist as a downloadable PDF, free and ungated, built from the same source as pages 2 to 4 and carrying the number.

## Build

Next.js with the App Router and Tailwind, deployed on Vercel, in a new repository under the forelandmarine organisation alongside website. Static rendering throughout, no database, no CMS, no client-side state beyond a navigation toggle.

Tokens defined once from the palette table above. Components kept to the minimum: page shell, section, table, callout, figure with caption, fee row, contact bar.

Budget the build at a few days, not weeks. The value is in the reference content, not the interface.

## Search and answer engines

The queries worth owning are operational and long-tail, which suits a small site with accurate content: superyacht agent London, yacht agent Thames, superyacht berth London, Tower Bridge lift superyacht, PLA pilotage yacht, sPCR superyacht, temporary admission superyacht UK, superyacht berth Southampton.

Structured data: Organization, Service and a FAQPage on the clearance page. Publish llms.txt and a full-text mirror, following the approach used for The First Owner's Reference.

On forelandmarine.com, add exactly one link, from the technical support page and the footer. Do not create a new top-level service page there, per the existing SEO constraint.

## What we are deliberately not doing

No crew placement, no charter brokerage, no chandlery.

No claim of UK and Ireland coverage.

No stock photography and no testimonials until they are real.

No supplier rebates and no percentage of the disbursement account.

No float. No supplier paid from Foreland money before the client's funds have cleared.
